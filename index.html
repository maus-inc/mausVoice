If you don't say it, you'll type it.

mausVoice turns your voice into text, anywhere you can type. Press a hotkey, speak, and the cleaned-up result lands in the app you're focused on.

Built in Rust for speed and a small footprint, with your keys encrypted on disk and a fully local Whisper mode for when your words never leave your machine.
