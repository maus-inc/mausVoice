Home is the default dashboard. It shows the current streak, words this month, lifetime words, recent dictations, and a daily activity heatmap for the past 53 calendar weeks. Dictation speed appears when recent saved recordings include audio-duration data.

The heatmap is the part people ask about, because its colour scale is compressed on purpose and its range is longer than it looks. This page reads it left to right.

The grid is a compact year view, not a calendar. One column is one week, one row is one weekday, and the last column is the current week. That is why a date can be found by counting columns from the right rather than by looking for a month label, because there are no month labels.

## Read a single day

<Steps>
  <Step
    title="Start from the weekday rail"
    image="home-step-1-week"
    alt="The weekday rail beside the week columns"
  >
    Monday is the top row. The rail labels Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday, and leaves the rows between them blank. The blank rows are still real days. A cell is never missing, it is only unlabelled, so a gap in the colour pattern means a day with no words, not a day that did not exist.
  </Step>
  <Step
    title="Hover a dated cell"
    image="home-step-2-read"
    alt="A heatmap cell scaled up with its date and word-count tooltip"
  >
    Hovering a dated cell scales it to 1.35 and outlines it for 150ms, and the tooltip states the date and the count in one line, for example "Jan 14, 2026: 312 words". Today's cell carries a standing outline, so the current day is findable without hovering. Touch has no hover state, and reduced motion removes the scale. The cells are informational, not controls, so they never take focus and never open a panel.
  </Step>
  <Step
    title="Read the legend, then hover for the exact count"
    image="home-step-3-summary"
    alt="A strip of heatmap cells above the Less to More legend"
  >
    The legend runs Less to More with three swatches, and the grid draws those same three tones: no words, 1 to 499 words, and 500 or more words. The scale compresses five count bands into three colours, so a day with 120 words and a day with 1,400 words share a tone. The colour shows shape, not magnitude. For the exact number, hover the cell and read the tooltip, or open History.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A date in the future is drawn as a transparent cell with no tooltip and no pointer events. It cannot be inspected, and it never looks busier than it is.

## Accessibility

The chart does not rely on colour alone. A visually hidden summary states the active days and the total words for the past year, the legend states the direction of the scale, and the same data is exposed as a list of daily word counts. A cell's description has the same shape as its tooltip, so a sighted reader and a screen-reader user get the same sentence.

## Layout

On a wide window the heatmap sits between two vertical metric stacks. On a narrower window the metrics move into two columns above the heatmap. The chart is part of the Home flow, not a separate side panel, and the shell does not add a duplicate hotkey card beside it.

The dashboard uses the available routed canvas without adding a shell-side panel. That is why resizing the window moves the metrics and leaves the chart in place rather than reflowing the whole page.

## Where the counts come from

Words are counted as the transcription is saved. Existing history can be backfilled once, and the backfill only walks records that existed before the feature, so a new recording is never counted twice. A dictation in Incognito mode skips the saved transcription row. When **Include incognito in stats** is on, the word count is still recorded on a best-effort basis, without saving the text. With the option off, the heatmap does not move.

<Callout title="What is stored">
  The dashboard keeps one row per local date with a word count. It never copies transcript text into the activity records. Deleting a transcription does not rewrite past totals, matching how the existing profile counters behave.
</Callout>
