# Anti-slop audit 002

Date: 2026-11-27
Scope: Issue #368 Settings layout changes and their responsive review. The product direction in `apps/desktop/DESIGN.md` and the core/UI/layout-mobile Antislop guidance were read. Audit mode: AFTER. The audit made no UI changes; the one selected finding was addressed afterward.

## Findings

1. **LOW — R-31, the 208px rail width has no explicit one-line rationale.** `apps/desktop/DESIGN.md` describes sidebars as approximately 224–240px, while `SettingsLayout.tsx` sets this rail to 208px at the wide container-query state. The surrounding comments explain why the rail responds to the Settings content area, but not why this exact narrower width is the right exception. If retained, document that it balances navigation and page-content width at the 680px container threshold; otherwise revisit the value.

## Checks that looked sound within this scope

- The 680px container-query transition corresponds to the actual Settings content area rather than the outer application width. Measurements across 800, 900, 934, 935, 936, 940, 950, and 1000px showed the 208px rail only at the 680px content threshold; before that the compact navigation wraps without horizontal scrolling.
- Shortcuts and AI and models were captured in light and dark at all eight widths. No horizontal page overflow, label/control intersections, console errors, or failed network requests were detected. The compact 800px and threshold-adjacent 935/936/940px states were optically inspected.
- All eight Settings navigation destinations were clicked and verified in both themes (16 route transitions); the search field returned two microphone results. No console errors or failed requests occurred.
- The 680px breakpoint, wrapped navigation, absence of horizontal scrolling, full-opacity placeholder color, and absence of the former viewport media breakpoint have regression assertions in `SettingsLayout.test.tsx`.
- Responsive captures and their geometry records are retained under `.qa-artifacts/issue-368/post-merge-responsive-review/`.
- No other finding was identified in the changed visual scope.

Finding 1 was approved and addressed as documented in `anti-slop/follow-up-002-2026-11-27.md`. No other finding numbers were selected.
