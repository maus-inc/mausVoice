# Anti-slop audit 002

Date: 2026-11-27  
Scope: Issue #365 Help page layout alignment (`HelpPage.tsx`, `TipCard.tsx`, tests, and preview verification harness). Existing product direction was read from `apps/desktop/DESIGN.md`.

## Design read

- Audience and task: Desktop users opening Help from the left navigation rail to review skipped first-run feature guides, jump to the matching feature or setting, or restore dismissed tips.
- Dials: `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 2`, `MOTION_INTENSITY: 1`, `VISUAL_DENSITY: 4` (quiet Operate-style utility surface aligned with `Dictionary`, `History`, `Styles`, `Apps`, and `Settings`).
- Surface and contrast posture: Active cards sit on flat `level1` surfaces over the `level0` canvas with a 1px solid hairline. Dismissed cards recede onto `level0` (`transparent`) with a 1px dashed hairline and softened title ink while keeping full opacity so all text stays above WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1) and interactive controls remain legible.

## Findings

1. **HIGH - Misaligned page frame and nested scrollport (`HelpPage.tsx`).** The prior Help page wrapped content in two bespoke `Box` layers with `maxWidth: 640`, left-aligned flush against the sidebar (`x: 244px` at 1280px vs centered `Container maxWidth="sm"` on sibling pages), and introduced a nested `overflow: auto` container inside `DashboardPage`'s scrollable `<main>`.

2. **HIGH - Broken secondary text contrast and flattened hierarchy (`HelpPage.tsx`, `TipCard.tsx`).** Both files passed `color="text.secondary"` as a Typography prop, which resolved to full primary ink (`rgb(26, 23, 18)` in light mode) in MUI v7 rather than `text.secondary`. Combined with `variant="h5"` on the page heading and uniform `spacing={2}` between heading, subtitle, and cards, the page lacked clear visual hierarchy.

3. **HIGH - Cramped dismissed cards and inconsistent internal alignment (`TipCard.tsx`, `HelpPage.tsx`).** Right-aligned side-by-side actions (`Browse styles` + `Show again`) squeezed dismissed card copy down to `211.5px` at `800x600` (5 wrapped lines, `177.8px` card height) and shifted action pills horizontally by over `110px` between cards. Leading glyphs centered vertically against multi-line copy instead of aligning to the title line, and inline buttons inherited `MuiButton.root`'s `44.3px` height.

4. **HIGH - Whole-card opacity dimmed dismissed cards below WCAG AA (`HelpPage.tsx`).** Applying `opacity: 0.65` to dismissed cards dropped secondary body copy to `2.52:1` in light mode and `3.76:1` in dark mode while dimming the `Show again` control.

5. **MEDIUM - Initial interactive probe covered only one theme per scenario.** Early verification exercised `Show again` only in light mode and assistant-on routing only in dark mode, leaving the reverse theme combinations unchecked in Chromium.

All five findings and their dispositions are recorded in `anti-slop/follow-up-002-2026-11-27.md`.
