# Wispr Flow

Source: docs.wisprflow.ai and wisprflow.ai. Crawled 2026-08-26.

Wispr Flow from Wispr AI (founded 2021, around $81M funding) is an AI dictation tool that covers Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android. System-wide dictation in every app, AI cleanup, Command Mode, and a Notetaker. Subscription tiers Free, Pro at $12 to $15 per month, and Enterprise.

## Platforms

- macOS 12 plus.
- Windows 10 64-bit plus.
- iOS 18.3 plus.
- Android 13 plus in beta.
- Only major tool on all four platforms.
- Cross-device sync of dictionary, styles, snippets, and settings.

## Core dictation

- System-wide into any app. Marketed at 4x faster than typing.
- Push-to-talk and hands-free with double-press activation.
- Clipboard fallback when no field is focused.
- Scratchpad on desktop and iOS. Whisper-quiet dictation.
- History plus dictation stats covering words, wpm, and streak.

## AI and LLM text processing

- Filler-word removal, auto punctuation and formatting, self-correction handling.
- Context-aware formatting adapts to the active app.
- Advanced AI thinking models on Pro.

## Editing and voice commands

- Command Mode on Mac and Windows paid: speak an instruction to transform selected text.
- Transforms in beta with up to 9 slots, 5 built-in rules plus 5 custom instructions, writing samples, Auto Apply After Dictation, and a Diff view.
- Polish on iOS with 5 built-in rules plus custom instructions.

## Personalization

- Personal dictionary that auto-adds corrections. Team dictionary on paid.
- Snippets are voice shortcuts with trigger up to 60 chars and body up to 4,000 chars. Available on desktop, iOS, and Android.
- Flow Styles and tone per app category (Formal, Casual, Excited). English-focused.
- Context Awareness reads limited on-screen text plus the active app for proper-noun and style matching.

## Wispr Notetaker for meeting capture

- On-device recording with no bot joining. Works with any meeting app and with in-person meetings.
- Live transcript. Re-reads the recording after the meeting for a sharper final plus speaker labels.
- Speaker identification by name. Flow Summaries. Ask Wispr chat on Mac only across meetings, notes, calendar, and apps.
- "What did I miss?" pre-read meeting briefs. Meeting detection and reminders.
- Calendar integration with Google only. Connectors for Gmail, Slack, and Notion. Share by link or email.
- Mac-only today. Windows coming soon. Included on Free and Pro.

## MCP integration

- Read-only MCP server exposes Notetaker data to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and VS Code.
- Quick-connect cards. Same URL across tools. Mac desktop only.

## Languages

- 100 plus marketed. 104 to 105 dictation languages. 12 confident.
- Auto language detection plus code-switching. One-click language picker.
- Styles and Context are English-focused. Notetaker is English-first.

## Privacy and security

- Privacy Mode with no retention or training. Opt-out of model training.
- Zero data retention agreements with providers.
- HIPAA on all plans. SOC 2 Type II. ISO 27001.
- Enterprise: SSO, SAML, SCIM, MDM, audit logs, admin controls.

## Pricing

- Free at $0 with 2,000 words per week on desktop, unlimited on Android. Notetaker and MCP included.
- Pro at $12 per month annual or $15 per month monthly. Unlimited dictation, thinking models, more meetings.
- Enterprise custom. Students 50 percent off. 40 percent accessibility discount. Android free at launch.

## Integrations and supported apps

- Dictation works in any text field: Gmail, Slack, Notion, code editors, AI tools.
- Notetaker connectors for Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, Notion.
- MCP-connected for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code.
- Meeting platforms: Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, Discord, browser, phone, in-person.

## Accessibility

- Hands-free tool for motor impairments, Parkinson's, RSI, dyslexia, ADHD, stuttering, and visual impairments. 40 percent disability discount.

## Developer

- No public REST API. The developer surface is the read-only remote MCP server with URL-based access and no keys.
- Up to 4 shortcuts per action. Mouse buttons 4 through 10.

## Known limitations

- Cloud-only with no offline mode. Screenshot and context capture raises privacy concerns.
- Trustpilot around 2.7 of 5.
- Windows app is heavy at around 800 MB of RAM.
- Command Mode, Transforms, and Context are gated to paid tiers or to Mac and Windows.
- Notetaker is Mac-only.
